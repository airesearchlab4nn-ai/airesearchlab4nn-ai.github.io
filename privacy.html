Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 November 2026

This policy covers (1) the Average Down Risk Simulator mobile app (package com.airesearchlab.averagedown) and (2) the YouTube tools that AI Research Lab uses to manage its own YouTube channel.

1. Average Down Risk Simulator app

The app does not collect, store on servers, or share any personal data. All inputs and calculations stay on your device. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising SDK and no network requests for your data.

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may process data about downloads and crashes under its own privacy policy; we do not receive personal data from it beyond the aggregated statistics Google Play Console shows to every developer.

If a future version adds optional features (for example email reminders or in-app purchases through Google Play or the App Store), this policy will be updated before release and those features will be opt-in.

2. AI Research Lab YouTube tools

AI Research Lab uses a private, internal application that connects to the YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and YouTube Reporting API (YouTube API Services) with Google sign-in (OAuth). It is used only by the lab’s operator, only for the lab’s own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and does not access other people’s accounts.

What it does
  • Uploads the lab’s own videos (as private first) and manages the lab’s own videos and playlists.
  • Reads statistics of the lab’s own channel and videos: views, likes, impressions, click-through rate, watch time and traffic sources (aggregated numbers, no viewer identities).
What it does not do
  • It does not read, collect or store personal data of viewers or of any other YouTube users.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

The sign-in token and the channel statistics are kept only on the lab’s own computer and its private backups. Aggregated statistics are used only to improve the lab’s own videos. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; after revocation the stored token stops working and is discarded.

By using YouTube, viewers are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service; Google processes data under the Google Privacy Policy. AI Research Lab’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. This website

This site is static HTML hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Children

The app and the lab’s content are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.
